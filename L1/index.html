AI Olympiad Training
KAUST Academy & Mawhiba

Level 1 (Beginners), September 19 to October 30, 2026.
All slides, labs and weekly competitions of the six-week programme, in one place.

PROGRAMME SCHEDULE

Weekly Breakdown

Week 1: Python & DS

Python from zero, the mathematics that AI runs on, and how to explore data with pandas and plots.

Week 2: ML Techniques

The machine learning toolbox: linear models, trees and ensembles, and how to judge a model honestly.

Week 3: NNs & DL

Neural networks from the perceptron to a full PyTorch training loop, and how to make training work.

Week 4: CV

Teaching computers to see: convolutions, CNNs, detection and segmentation, augmentation and best practices.

Week 5: Transfer Learning & Embeddings

Standing on giant models: pretrained encoders, fine-tuning, embeddings, similarity and CLIP.

Week 6: NLP

From text to tensors: tokenization, text classifiers, RNNs, attention, transformers and BERT.
